Our camera rules

These rules bind every camera on this site. They sit inside the principles of PrompTugal.news: accurate, never presented as other than it is, sources and licences shown, mistakes corrected in the open.

  1. Public cameras only. Only cameras published by public bodies (câmaras municipais, Infraestruturas de Portugal, ports, airports, parks) or by operators who offer an official embed or a public page. Each camera is on data.promptugal.news's register, with its operator's terms checked first.
  2. Only cameras we can embed, in the operator's own player. We show a camera on the map and in the list only where its operator offers an embed whose terms let us use it, from the operator's own site, and we have reviewed that host; each view carries the operator's name and a link to them. A registered camera without such an embed waits apart, linked to its operator, while we ask them. We never re-host, record, download or store frames, snapshots or streams.
  3. No analysis of what a camera shows. Never face, person or number-plate recognition, and no other analysis of the pictures.
  4. Never an unsecured private camera. A camera reachable only because it is unsecured or still on a default password (the kind listed on sites such as Insecam) is not public, and we never list it. Never police, traffic-enforcement or private CCTV.
  5. Metadata only. What we keep: the camera, its operator, its place and coordinates, its terms, its licence link, whether it was up or down and when (its status history), and the weather in that place from data.promptugal.news.
  6. Take-down on request, and claims. If you run a camera listed here, or think we should not list it, open an issue or use promptugal.news/report/: we take the entry down while we talk. An operator can claim its entry the same way and correct it.
